# Changelog

Every build that leaves this Mac has a section here and a tag `mobile-v<version>`. Builds before 1.1.1+3 were made
before the app was in git and are not recorded.

## 1.2.0+4 (24 Nov 2026)

- Account managers send the first email to a prospect from the app: Playbook & email on a prospect shows how to
  approach them and what to sell, then the email (their saved draft or the playbook template) to edit, save or send.
  It goes out under their name, their replies come to the account manager, and a follow-up is booked.

## 1.1.1+3 (19 Nov 2026)

- Screens follow each person's role, decided by the server; a role with no screens sees "Nothing to show for your
  role yet" with Refresh instead of crashing.
- Sales get the CRM screens.
- Repair photos no longer break on empty photo lists.
- Faster: answers kept for 45 seconds, pull to refresh always asks the server.
- Published on scharge.sharet.africa/downloads (scharge-app-arm64.apk, sha256 17a5ea4c…).
